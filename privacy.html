JARVIS: privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026.

Who uses it

Only the owner's own Google account. JARVIS is a personal tool and has no other users.

What it can access

Gmail, read-only (the gmail.readonly permission). JARVIS cannot send, delete, move or label mail, and it does not mark mail as read.

Where the data goes

  • JARVIS runs on the owner's Mac. It keeps no copy of the mailbox, and no server of ours stores mail.
  • When the owner asks about an email, its text is sent to the AI model service the owner uses, so that it can answer. Mail from banks and health providers is not sent.
  • Alerts about missed mail carry only the sender and the subject, and go to the owner alone.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove JARVIS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
